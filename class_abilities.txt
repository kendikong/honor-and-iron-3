Tactical RPG Class Design Document (Gloomhaven Syntax)
Game Concept, Core Mechanics & Scaling Philosophy
Genre & Engine: A tactical, grid-based RPG focused on deterministic math, positional physics (push/pull collisions, traps), and tight, puzzle-like combat encounters.
Skill & Passive Design Criteria
When designing or balancing any Active Skill or Passive ability, it MUST meet the following five criteria:
Adherence to Core Pillars: It must strictly follow Zero RNG, Perfect Information, Deterministic Math, and the Universal Scaling Mandate.
Player Engagement (Fun Factor): Mechanics must be inherently interesting, satisfying to execute, and create fun "Aha!" tactical moments for the player.
Build Synergy: Every skill and passive must directly synergize with the core identity of one or more Advanced Class builds.
Universal Utility & Balance: Abilities must remain useful and mathematically viable across the early, mid, and end game. Highly niche skills that only function in rare, highly specific scenarios are strictly prohibited.
Thematic Consistency: Passives and active skills MUST perfectly fit the narrative fantasy and physical reality of the build/class. Gritty, physical classes should never cast magic or cleanse debuffs. Heavy, armored classes should not dodge or leap. Every mechanic must make logical, in-world sense.
Global Rules First (Skill Implementation Mandate): Every skill MUST follow the game's global rules and shared systems as much as possible — economy columns, targeting modes, effect types, timeline behavior, preview/simulation path, and data-driven authoring. Do NOT invent per-skill engine branches, hidden timeline slots, or one-off validation unless the Master Bible explicitly requires it. If a skill truly cannot be expressed without a NEW global rule or system exception, STOP and warn the project owner BEFORE implementing: state what breaks, what new rule you propose, and ask whether it is acceptable. Do not ship silent exceptions.
Scaling, Constitution & HP Design Philosophy
Constitution Stat (CON) & HP Calculation: Base HP is strictly derived from the Constitution (CON) stat, where 1 CON = 5 HP. Direct static HP values are deprecated. This tightly couples survivability to a core stat and enforces predictable scaling logic.
Promotion Boosts Notation: `(+X STAT, 0 MOV)` means the promotion grants no movement bonus. It does not set MOV to 0.
Keyword Parity Mandate: All in-game ability descriptors, UI labels, and Tooltips must strictly emulate the mechanical keywords established in this Master Bible (e.g., ATK X, HEAL X, PUSH X, Apply TAUNT, AOE 3). Conversational English descriptions are forbidden unless explicitly providing required spatial or mechanical clarity.
CRITICAL AI INSTRUCTION: Lines in the global rules that name a class skill (Shield Slam, Phalanx Stance, Holy Light, Density Shift, Repelling Shot, etc.) are illustrations of complete / upgraded behavior. They are not a second skill list and do not override the class section's base vs upgrade split. The class skill line is the definition.
CRITICAL AI INSTRUCTION: The HP stat must remain a highly rewarding investment for the player. When designing or tweaking movesets, adhere to the following strict mathematical rules:
The Universal Scaling Mandate: All sources of damage, damage mitigation, healing, and shielding MUST scale reasonably as the game progresses. Do not create static "deal 2 damage" or "reduce damage by 1" mechanics. Every value must tie into a stat multiplier (STR/MAG/DEF), a Max HP percentage, Weapon Might (WPN), or an escalating 'X' variable.
The Flat Cost Exception: Fixed numbers are strictly reserved for deliberate economic costs. For example, a Bruiser spending 5 HP for a buff feels risky at Level 1, but becomes a rewarding, trivial cost at Level 10. Do not convert self-damage to percentages.
Do NOT use percentage-based damage for DoTs, Hazards, or Self-Damage. Flat damage punishes low-HP units and rewards high-HP tanks for their stat investment.
The Tank-Buster Exception (POISON): POISON is the sole deliberate exception to the flat-damage rule, dealing percentage-based Max HP damage to explicitly counter massive health pools.
Status Effects & Hazards Scale via the "X" Variable. (e.g., An Act 1 Goblin applies BURN 2. An Act 3 Dragon applies BURN 5). Player-applied hazards scale off their WPN or MAG stats.
Utility & Defenses MUST use Percentages. SHIELD X and the health pools of Summons/Constructs scale off Max HP (or higher of CON/MAG for magic construct classes). This allows HP to moonlight as a scaling "Magic/Utility" stat for builders and supports.
Heals Are Underpowered: Healing should never trivialize tactical errors. Passive heals are strictly capped at 10% (HEAL 1) or 20% (HEAL 2), pushing players to rely on proactive mitigation (SHIELD, positioning) over infinite sustain.
Boss CC Immunity & Hard CC Fallbacks: Bosses are inherently immune to Hard Crowd Control (STAGGER, PACIFY, FEAR, CONFUSION, ROOT, and SILENCE). Bosses also cannot be instantly killed by Chasms; instead, they snap to the nearest valid tile and suffer 5 unmitigated damage. Every Hard CC application spends the fallback below unless the skill writes a custom Boss Fallback. Default Boss Fallback: ROOT → −2 MOV for 1 turn (does not break on damage); STAGGER → +WPN unmitigated on that hit or collision; SILENCE → cannot gain SHIELD for 1 turn; FEAR → PUSH 2 away from source; CONFUSION → BLIND for 1 turn; PACIFY → −2 ATK (min 0) for 1 turn. Custom clauses (Shield Bash, Terrify, EMP vs Mechanical Bosses) override the default. Note: WITHER is a Soft CC debuff and affects Bosses at 25% damage output reduction instead of 50%.
Max HP Buff Expiration: Reductions in Max HP from expiring temporary buffs reduce Current HP to 1 instead of causing death. Intentionally harmful effects kill normally.
The 1-Phase Simultaneous Turn System
Combat uses a single, streamlined planning phase for the player, followed by an execution phase, and then the Enemy Turn.
Action Economy: 1 AP is gained at the start of the turn (varies for advanced classes). Executing a standard basic attack costs 0 AP. Active Skills cost 1 AP unless explicitly stated otherwise. A 0 AP Active Skill still consumes the Action slot unless its text says it does not consume the Action slot. Class Reposition Skills cost MOV (see each class's Reposition Skill line) — never AP. Active Movement Skills cost AP or MOV depending on skill text. Run costs 1 AP and does not consume the Action slot by itself; pairing Run with a walk is a Pre-Move action. AP granted by skills (On Kill refunds, Iron Grip, Elemental Surge, and so on) is added to the unit's AP pool and is not discarded because this turn's Action was already spent. Extra Active Skills in the same turn require future Max AP / extra Action economy; until then, leftover AP remains for the next turn. Global AP refund cap: a unit may refund at most 1 AP per turn from skills and passives combined, unless a skill line names a different cap. Refunds that would exceed the cap are not granted (Mana Siphon converts the blocked refund into MAG heal + Arcane Overchannel as written on that line).
The 1-Input Turn: During the planning phase, a player may plot a sequence to move up to their Max MOV and perform exactly 1 Skill (or basic attack).
Movement Timing: Movement can be plotted to occur entirely before the action, OR entirely after the action. Standard movement cannot be split unless modified by an Innate Trait or skill effect.
Phase Execution & Physics: When the player ends their planning phase, all planned actions execute mathematically simultaneously. The animation sequence resolves as:
Pre-action movements and Reposition Skills.
Skill actions/attacks (HP reduced to 0 does not remove entity yet; 0 HP entities remain targetable and interactive until step 5).
Post-action movements (and CANTO movements).
Forced movement (PUSH/PULL) vectors are added together and resolved last.
Death cleanup (0 HP entities removed).
Simultaneous Kill Resolution (Highest Damage + Tie-Breakers): If multiple actions reduce a unit's HP to 0 during the same turn execution, kill credit and all associated "On Kill" passive triggers are awarded deterministically to a single unit using the following hierarchy:
Highest Damage: The unit that dealt the single largest amount of damage to the target during that phase gets the kill credit.
Proximity: If damage is tied, the attacking unit closest in Manhattan distance to the target at the time of execution receives the kill credit.
Grid Order: If distance is also tied, the unit closest to board origin (Tile 0,0) receives the kill credit.
Note: The planning UI's ghost preview explicitly indicates which unit is slated to receive kill credit.
Physics & Elevation Matrix (Airborne vs Grounded): Forced movement (PUSH/PULL) respects vertical elevation state (AIRBORNE vs Grounded):
An AIRBORNE unit ignores ground hazards, traps, Chasms, and collisions with grounded entities while moving.
Collisions only occur between entities sharing the same physical plane (Grounded vs Grounded, or AIRBORNE vs AIRBORNE).
Pushing an AIRBORNE unit over a Chasm does not execute a drop unless the AIRBORNE status expires mid-movement.
Dynamic Intents: All queued actions are fully cancelable until execution. Enemy intents dynamically update in real-time on the UI in response to every player's plotted action.
Universal Action Economy & Planning Timeline (Authoritative Rules)
Resource Types:
Movement Points (MOV / MP): Spent only on movement — basic tile walks, class Reposition Skills, and any ability whose cost is listed as MOV.
Action Points (AP): Spent only on class Active Skills and Run. Basic Attack costs 0 AP.
Ability Classification (every ability in the game belongs to exactly one category):
Class Active Skill — Costs AP (unless explicitly 0 AP). Consumes the unit's one Action for the turn. Appears in the Action timeline column. Includes Basic Attack (0 AP) and Active Movement Skills (Dashes, Charges, Pounces).
Class Reposition Skill — Costs MOV (not AP). Does NOT consume the Action slot. Appears in the Pre-Move timeline column. Always granted per class; never part of the rolled skill pool. When a Reposition Skill targets a unit, it may only target an ally (never an enemy). Exception: Pullback moves the unit occupying the tile directly in front of you, ally or enemy.
Universal Run — Not a class skill. SELF. Costs 1 AP. Does not consume the Action slot. Grants +Floor(50% of Max MOV) extra movement for this turn's walk. Plotted in the Pre-Move column with a walk (Auto-Run when the path exceeds the walk budget). One Run per turn.
Universal Wait — Not a skill and not shown in the skill list. Invoked via the Wait control / double-click unit row. Forcibly exhausts the unit for the turn (no Action, no Post-Move / CANTO) even if movement remains. Stored in a hidden exhaustion slot (not displayed as a fourth timeline column).
Planning Timeline Columns (displayed):
Pre-Move: Basic walks (drag path), Run-boosted walks, and class Reposition Skills — in committed order (e.g. Move(3,1) -> Swap(ally)).
Action: Class Active Skills and Basic Attack (0 AP).
Post-Move: Remaining movement after the Action (including CANTO refunds).
Pre-Move Commit & Cancel:
Pre-Move steps apply immediately during planning (live board + ghost preview) and are individually cancelable.
Canceling a Reposition Skill that moved/swapped an ally also cancels any of that ally's planned actions that depended on the new positions after that step.
Targeting Data (skill list must show):
Each ability declares targeting mode (Self, Ally, Enemy, Tile, etc.), MOV or AP cost, range, and effect keywords — players must not infer behavior from effect-type heuristics.
Targeting Assignment (Planning Cursor — Authoritative):
Four rules cover almost all skills. The red range bubble is preview only — it does not mean tile targeting.
1. RANGE 0 or SELF keyword → self targeting (no aim step). Includes RANGE 0 | AOE … self-centered blasts (Seismic Stomp, Cleansing Aura, etc.).
2. Movement → tile targeting by default (MOVE, DASH, TELEPORT, jump to empty tile, Sidestep, trap/hazard placement, summon obstacle, item/corpse tiles, AOE / ARC / CONE / SKEWER / GLOBAL area picks). Exception: when the skill is specifically designed to work on allies or enemies only, use unit targeting even if the effect is movement (e.g. Reposition Skill Swap on ally; Shadow Step to enemy; Swift Strike MOVE to target; Guardian Step warp to ally; Glorious Charge pick ally; Switcheroo / Shadow Swap / Meat Shield swap with a unit).
3. Single ally or single enemy at range — and not movement, not an area footprint → unit targeting (plain RANGE X | ATK / HEAL / debuff / mark on one unit). Never TILE on these.
Dual-faction single picks (Holy Light heal ally or harm enemy; Density Shift ally or enemy) are still unit targeting. Repelling Shot is authored enemy-first; ally push is a special case.
4. Dual-pick skills (Usher's ally then empty tile; Voodoo Link / Martyr's Chains two enemies; Sympathetic Bond ally and enemy; Glorious Charge ally then enemy): first pick is the unit named first; second pick is the other unit or empty tile named in the skill.
Conditional Damage (data-driven, not per-skill code):
These lines show complete upgraded behavior as examples for shared systems. Base vs upgrade splits are in the class sections.
Shield Slam (upgraded): ATK 2 | PUSH 2 | If target was already adjacent at cast, gain ATK +2. Target DEF is reduced by 1 before damage is calculated.
Phalanx Stance (upgraded): Grants RETALIATION_INFINITE_RANGE for Retaliation Protocol this turn (counter range becomes map-wide while both effects apply).
Combat Math, Enemy Scaling & TTK Philosophy
The Core Formulas
Raw Physical Damage = (Skill Base Power (ATK X) + WPN) * (1 + STR / 5)
Raw Magical Damage = (MAG ATK + WPN) * (1 + MAG / 5)
Effective Weapon Damage (Weaknesses) = STR + (WPN * 3)
Final Physical Damage = Max(0, Raw Physical Damage - Enemy DEF)
Final Magical Damage = Max(0, Raw Magical Damage - Floor((Enemy DEF + Enemy MAG) / 2))
Multi-Hit Mitigation: Enemy defenses mitigate each individual hit of a multi-hit attack separately according to its unique property.
The "Weaker" Rounding Rule: Whenever fractional math occurs (like a 50% reduction or multiplier), always round in the direction that makes the outcome weaker for the caster/player (e.g., Round down your damage output, healing, and summon health. Round up the enemy's remaining armor).
Symmetrical Enemy Design
Enemies are constructed using the exact same stat ratio rules as player classes.
Standard Grunts: Mirror the "Mercenary" class (Moderate HP, Low DEF).
Tank Enemies: Mirror the "Knight" class (Moderate HP, High DEF).
Percentage-Based Global Scaling
To increase difficulty across acts, stages, or "Hard Modes," we never hand-tune individual enemy stats. Instead, we apply a Global Percentage Multiplier to their base Level 1 symmetrical stats (e.g., Act 2 uses a 300% multiplier on base stats).
Keyword Terminology Glossary
Action & Utility Keywords
ATK X: Deal physical damage scaling off the skill's base power, weapon might, and your strength (X = Skill Base Power).
MAG ATK X: Deal magical damage scaling off the skill's base power, weapon might, and your magic (X = Skill Base Power).
HEAL X: Restore health equal to Floor(X * 10% of target's Max HP).
MAG HEAL X: Restore health equal to Floor((X + WPN) * (1 + MAG / 5) * 0.20 + 20% of Target's Max HP). Used strictly by dedicated support classes to scale healing off both their MAG stat and the target's HP.
SHIELD X: Gain temporary over-HP equal to Floor(X * 10% of target's Max HP). Takes priority over normal HP.
SHIELD equal to STAT: Raw over-HP equal to that stat (DEF, missing HP, Scrap, etc.). This is not the SHIELD X formula.
Targeting Keywords (Manhattan Distance)
RANGE X: Maximum target distance in tiles. Line of sight required unless otherwise specified.
RANGE 0: Anchored to the caster's current tile (Self).
MOVE X: Move up to X tiles.
AOE XxX: A square array of tiles (e.g., 2x2, 3x3).
AOE X: Cross-shaped, expands X tiles from the center.
ARC: 3-tile sweep attack (1x3 or 3x1 perpendicular line).
CONE X: A directional arc expanding outward from the caster for X tiles.
SKEWER X: X tiles in one cardinal direction.
DASH X: Move X tiles in one cardinal direction.
GLOBAL: Ignores range limits and line of sight.
Status Effects & Mechanics (The Flat "X" Scaling)
BURN X: Take exactly X unmitigated damage at the start of the turn. Leaves FIRE terrain if moving.
BLEED X: Take exactly X unmitigated damage at the end of the turn. Moving costs +1 MOV per tile.
POISON: Take unmitigated damage equal to 10% of Max HP (rounded up) at the start of the turn. Healing received is reduced by 50% (rounded down).
WEAKEN: Target suffers -2 STR and -2 MAG for the duration.
VULNERABLE: Target loses all Push Mitigation (pushed maximum distance by all collisions next turn) and cannot gain SHIELD.
STAGGER: Target's Action Points (AP) are reduced by 1 for their next turn.
ROOT: Target's MOVEMENT becomes 0. Effect breaks instantly if target takes any damage after the ROOT is already on them. Status effects from an attack apply after that attack's damage (a damaging pin still applies ROOT). Can still attack if in range.
SILENCE: Cannot cast Active Skills. Can only use standard Movement and basic physical attacks.
TAUNT: Target is forced to target the Taunter with their next offensive action.
BLIND: Target's maximum RANGE is reduced to 1.
PACIFY: Target cannot use offensive skills or basic attacks for duration. Breaks instantly on damage.
FEAR: Target must spend entire MOVEMENT running directly away from source on next turn.
CONFUSION: Target is forced to target nearest unit (friend or foe) other than caster.
WITHER: Target's total damage output is reduced by 50% (rounded down), maximum MOVEMENT is reduced by -2 MOV, and target cannot gain SHIELD or positive buffs for the duration. Against Bosses, the damage output reduction is reduced to 25%.
PIERCE: Attack ignores target's defensive mitigations entirely.
GHOST: Unit can move through enemy-occupied tiles and traps/hazards without penalty (intangible).
TRAMPLE: Unit can move through enemy tiles. Dependent on skill text, movement through an enemy triggers effect as a "hit".
STEALTH: Unit cannot be targeted by ranged attacks or skills (Range > 1), unless the skill names a different range (e.g. Range > 3).
INTERCEPT: Redirect a percentage of incoming damage from a designated ally to this unit. Default: that ally must be adjacent, unless the skill names a longer range.
MARK: Attackers deal extra damage equal to the attacker's WPN against this target. Lasts 2 turns unless the skill says otherwise. Skills may add extra MARK riders (RANGE, PIERCE, etc.).
DEBUFFED: The unit has any negative status (ROOT, STAGGER, BLEED, POISON, WEAKEN, MARK, FEAR, and so on) OR is inside a hostile stat-penalty aura (Hexing Presence, Intimidating Presence). Aura penalties count.
ISOLATED: A unit with no adjacent ally (no other unit of the same faction in an adjacent tile). An enemy standing next to another enemy is not isolated.
SMOKE: Tile field. Units inside cannot be targeted by attackers standing outside (STEALTH vs outside). Lasts as the skill says.
CALTROPS: Trap tile. Entering applies the placing skill's listed effects. If the skill names ATK X, that is the entry damage. If it names ROOT / BLEED / DEF shred and no ATK, those apply with no ATK (Caltrop Trap is ROOT + BLEED; Caltrop Toss is ATK).
THORNS X%: When hit in melee, the attacker takes Floor(X% of the damage they dealt), rounded down.
Push Mitigation: How much PUSH/PULL distance is reduced. VULNERABLE sets it to 0. Density Shift can double it.
CLEANSE: Instantly remove all negative debuffs and status effects.
PURGE: Instantly remove all positive buffs and shields.
STURDY: Immune to PUSH and PULL effects.
INVULNERABLE: Immune to all damage, debuffs, and positional manipulation.
HOLY AURA: 1-tile aura projected by an object. Allies in the aura gain +1 DEF. Enemies that start their turn in the aura suffer MAG ATK 1 (caster's MAG, object as origin).
LIGHTNING ROD: Magical attacks that hit a unit in RANGE 2 of this object also deal MAG ATK 1 (owner's MAG) to all enemies adjacent to the object (once per attack).
AIRBORNE: Standard movement ignores all ground hazards, traps, difficult terrain, and Chasms.
SHARED TILE (Airlift): While carried, the ally occupies the rider's tile. Both remain on the board. Each occupant can still be selected as a unit target. Melee RANGE 1 and any AOE that includes that tile hit both occupants. The carried ally cannot spend MOV or use their Action until dropped. They do not block the rider's movement. Drop requires an empty tile.
CANTO: After executing a Skill or basic attack, Max MOV is fully refunded for remaining movement.
OVERCLOCK: A friendly Construct immediately triggers its once-per-turn effect again this phase.
TRAMPLED: Difficult terrain (MOVE cost x2) created by a charge. Lasts 1 turn unless specified.
PUSH/PULL COLLISIONS: If pushed into an obstacle or unit, pushed unit deals collision damage but "bounces" back to original tile without either entity changing tiles. If two units are pushed into exact same destination tile simultaneously, both suffer collision damage, bounce back, and stop 1 tile early.
Summon & Obstacle Scaling Rules
To ensure board-control classes remain viable in the late game, destructible entities dynamically scale with the caster's Max HP (or higher of CON/MAG) upon creation (minimum 1 HP). All constructs are inherently STURDY. If stated as "once per turn", they act simultaneously at the very end of the Player Execution Phase, right before the Enemy Turn begins.
Construct Turret (Engineer): HP equals Floor(50% of Caster's Max HP).
Turret Syndrome Mini-Turret (Engineer): HP equals Floor(25% of Caster's Max HP).
Tesla Barricade (Engineer): HP equals Floor(150% of Caster's Max HP).
Magnetic Mine (Engineer): HP equals Floor(25% of Caster's Max HP).
Voodoo Totems (Shaman): HP equals Floor(50% of Caster's Max HP).
Bone Barricade (Shaman): HP equals Floor(50% of Caster's Max HP).
Obsidian Wall (Mage): HP equals Floor(50% of Caster's Max HP).
Divine Hammer (Cleric): HP equals Floor(25% of Caster's Max HP).

1. Knight
Core Identity: Tank-Strong, High DEF, Low Mobility. The ultimate shield. Focuses on total damage absorption, shielding, and becoming an indestructible bastion.
| CON 6 | MOV 3 | STR 4 | DEF 5 | MAG 2 |
Innate Trait: Bastion Front
Base Mechanic: The Knight takes 0 collision damage when pushed or knocked into walls, obstacles, or units. Additionally, all incoming physical damage originating directly from the 180-degree frontal arc (3 frontal tiles relative to facing direction) is reduced by an extra +2 DEF.
Upgrade: Frontal damage mitigation increased by an additional +2 DEF (total +4 DEF from front). When a collision occurs involving the Knight (as the shoved unit or as the blocker), the Knight gains SHIELD 2 for 1 turn.
Reposition Skill: Swap (Cost: 1 MOV) — Select an adjacent ally. Swap positions with them.
Upgrade: The Knight immediately gains +2 DEF and SHIELD 2 for the rest of the turn.
Advanced Classes (Promotion Boosts)
The Sentinel: (+4 DEF, +4 CON, 0 MOV) Maximize damage mitigation, shield walls, and near-death failsafes.
The Juggernaut: (+4 STR, +4 DEF, 0 MOV) Maximize retaliation damage by pulling enemies into you.
The Cataphract: (+2 CON, +2 DEF, +2 MOV) Gains a heavy draft horse. Focuses on rushing to project ally buffs and intercepting damage.
Passives
(The Juggernaut) Kinetic Dissipation: When you are shoved into an obstacle or unit, OR when you block a collision (an enemy is shoved into you), absorb kinetic momentum to gain SHIELD equal to your DEF and trigger a 1-tile radial shockwave dealing physical damage equal to DEF to adjacent enemies. Upgrade: The enemy is also PUSHED 1 tile after collision.
(The Juggernaut) Thorny Carapace: When an enemy hits you in melee, they reflect 50% damage back (rounded down) and are PUSHED 1 tile. Upgrade: Reflects 100% damage instead.
(The Juggernaut) Concussive Shatter: Enemy collision suffers extra damage equal to 50% of your DEF, and target loses DEF equal to your WPN. Upgrade: Target also suffers VULNERABLE.
(The Juggernaut) Stand Ground: You cannot be displaced by PUSH or PULL (you stay on your tile). The shove still resolves as a collision against you, so blocker collision passives still trigger. Enemies attempting to shove you suffer ATK 1. Upgrade: They suffer ATK 2 instead.
(The Juggernaut) Kinetic Momentum: Causing an enemy to suffer collision damage grants SHIELD equal to your STR + DEF for 1 turn. Upgrade: Also refunds 1 MOV point on your first collision each turn.
(The Sentinel) Indestructible Bastion: If taking lethal damage, survive at 1 HP and gain SHIELD equal to your DEF (Once per combat). Upgrade: Gain +2 STR for the rest of combat when this triggers.
(The Sentinel) Phalanx Deflection: When mitigating frontal damage with DEF or SHIELD, store 50% of the mitigated damage as Kinetic Energy (capped at 2 * DEF). Your next active melee skill or counter-attack consumes all stored energy to deal bonus physical damage equal to the stored amount and apply PUSH 1. Upgrade: Capped at 3 * DEF.
(The Sentinel) Kinetic Armor: Incoming damage is reduced by Floor(DEF / 2) if any SHIELD is active. Upgrade: Incoming damage is reduced by Floor((DEF + 2) / 2) instead.
(The Sentinel) Kinetic Converter: When Hit, gain +1 STR and +1 MOV for next turn. Upgrade: Gain +2 STR instead.
(The Sentinel) Kinetic Redirection: Whenever you successfully mitigate enemy damage using your DEF or SHIELD, your next attack this combat gains +1 STR (Stacks up to +3). Stacks reset to 0 after declaring an attack. Upgrade: Next attack also gains PIERCE.
(The Cataphract) Bulwark: Gain +1 DEF for every adjacent enemy or ally. Upgrade: Also gain +1 STR per adjacent enemy.
(The Cataphract) Living Barricade: Allies directly behind you are immune to ranged attacks. Upgrade: Allies directly behind you also gain +1 DEF.
(The Cataphract) Shield Wall: Adjacent allies gain +1 DEF and immunity to PULL. Upgrade: Range of aura increases to 2 tiles.
(The Cataphract) Rallying Presence: Allies who start their turn adjacent to you gain +1 MOV for that turn. Upgrade: Movement bonus increases to +2.
(The Cataphract) Intercept Tactics: When using a skill to redirect damage, gain +2 DEF. Upgrade: Gain +3 DEF instead.
Active Skills & Upgrades
(The Juggernaut) Shield Bash: RANGE 1 | ATK 1 | PUSH 2 Upgrade: Apply STAGGER if target collides with a wall or enemy. (Boss Fallback: If immune to STAGGER, deal +WPN bonus damage on collision).
(The Sentinel) Phalanx Stance: SELF | DEF +5 | Gain STURDY until next turn. Upgrade: Gain infinite RANGE for "Retaliation Protocol" this turn.
(The Juggernaut / The Cataphract) Taunting Strike: RANGE 2 | ATK 1 | PULL 1 | Apply TAUNT. Upgrade: RANGE 3 | 3x3 square centered on the target | PULL 2 all enemies in that square toward the target | TAUNT those enemies.
(The Cataphract) Seismic Stomp: RANGE 0 | AOE 1 | ATK 2 | PURGE all enemies. Upgrade: Create CRACKED terrain (MOVE cost x2).
(The Cataphract / The Sentinel) Fortify: RANGE 3 | Target Ally's DEF is increased by your current DEF for 1 turn (added on top of their DEF, not replaced). Upgrade: Target gains THORNS 50% (Reflects half damage back to attacker, rounded down).
(The Juggernaut) Bowling Charge: DASH 3 | On collision: ATK 3, PUSH 2. Upgrade: Target collision with another enemy: ATK 3 to both units.
(The Sentinel / The Juggernaut) Iron Grip: RANGE 1 | Apply ROOT. Target DEF halved next turn (rounded up). Upgrade: Refund 1 AP if target already has ROOT or STAGGER.
(The Cataphract / The Sentinel) Redirect Strike: RANGE 2 | Target Ally. You INTERCEPT 50% of damage dealt to that ally this turn (rounded down), even if they are not adjacent. Upgrade: Gain +2 DEF for every redirected hit.
(The Sentinel) Indomitable Will: SELF | Gain SHIELD equal to your missing HP (raw over-HP, not the SHIELD X formula) for 2 turns. Upgrade: When SHIELD expires, gain +2 STR.
(The Juggernaut) Retaliation Protocol: SELF | Until next turn, when hit in melee, counter-attack for ATK 2. Upgrade: Counter-attacks apply PUSH 1.
(The Sentinel) Shield Slam: RANGE 1 | ATK 2 | PUSH 2 | If target was already adjacent, gain ATK +2. Upgrade: Target DEF is reduced by 1 before damage is calculated.
(The Cataphract / The Sentinel) Defensive Formation: RANGE 0 | AOE 3 | Allies in range gain +2 DEF and become immune to PUSH/PULL for 1 turn. Upgrade: Allies also gain SHIELD 2.
(The Juggernaut) Chain Hook: RANGE 3 | ATK 1 | PULL 2. Upgrade: Target suffers VULNERABLE if pulled adjacent.
(The Cataphract) Trampling Advance: MOVE 2 | ATK 2 | PUSH 1. Upgrade: Gain SHIELD 1 per tile moved.

2. Bruiser
Core Identity: Tanky Melee-Strong, High HP. Dynamic HP management. Can play as a low-HP berserker or a high-HP behemoth that uses their mass as a weapon.
| CON 7 | MOV 4 | STR 4 | DEF 2 | MAG 1 |
Innate Trait: Sanguine Regeneration
Base Mechanic: At the start of every turn during resolution, the Bruiser automatically restores health equal to Floor(5% of Max HP).
Upgrade: Turn-start regeneration increases to Floor(10% of Max HP). If the Bruiser is already at Max HP when this triggers, the excess healing is converted directly into temporary SHIELD.
Reposition Skill: Push Through (Cost: 2 MOV) — Move into an adjacent tile occupied by an ally, pushing them 1 tile forward.
Upgrade: Cost reduced to 1 MOV. Pushing a unit grants the Bruiser +1 STR for their next attack this turn.
Advanced Classes (Promotion Boosts)
The Bloodrager: (+4 STR, +4 CON, 0 MOV) Low HP management with high STR buffs and self-healing.
The Behemoth: (+6 CON, +2 DEF, 0 MOV) High HP scaling. Uses massive health pool and body weight to crush targets and pierce defenses.
The Siegebreaker: (+4 STR, +2 CON, +2 MOV) High momentum. Discards defensive gear to sprint at top speed and shatter enemy lines.
Passives
(The Bloodrager) Reactive Adrenaline: Starting your turn adjacent to 1+ enemies converts your turn-start Sanguine Regeneration heal into instant SHIELD equal to the heal amount instead, AND grants +1 STR for every adjacent enemy (max +3 STR). Upgrade: Also grants +1 DEF per adjacent enemy.

The Mechanist: (+4 CON, +2 DEF, +2 MOV) Pneumatic exo-suit legs for mobility, repair, and overclocking.
Passives
(The Siegecrafter) Turret Syndrome: End turn without moving -> spawn mini-turret adjacent (Floor(25% Caster Max HP)). Upgrade: Turret gains +50% Max HP.
(The Siegecrafter) Automation: Turrets gain ATK +1 and RANGE +1. Upgrade: Turrets gain ATK +2.
(The Siegecrafter) Master Builder: +1 active construct limit. Upgrade: +2 active construct limit.
(The Siegecrafter) Reinforced Constructs: Constructs gain +25% Max HP and inherit 50% of your DEF. Upgrade: Constructs gain +50% Max HP and inherit 100% of your DEF.
(The Siegecrafter) Shield Generator: Allies adjacent to turrets gain +1 DEF. Upgrade: Immunity to PULL.
(The Demolitionist) Blast Shielding: Immune to own explosion damage. Upgrade: Explosion hitting 3+ enemies grants +1 AP (1/turn).
(The Demolitionist) Explosive Expert: Explosives deal extra damage equal to WPN to mechanicals and ignore DEF. Upgrade: Explosions gain ATK +2.
(The Demolitionist) Chain Reaction: Construct detonation triggers Manual Detonation on friendly explosives in RANGE 2. Upgrade: RANGE 3.
(The Demolitionist) Shrapnel: Device detonations apply BLEED X (X = WPN) and PUSH 1. Upgrade: Also applies BLIND.
(The Demolitionist) Expanded Blast: Explosion AOE increased by +1 tile. Upgrade: Explosions destroy traps/cover.
(The Mechanist) Scrap Mechanic: Enemy dying in RANGE 3 drops Scrap (upgrades construct HP). Upgrade: Drops 2 Scrap.
(The Mechanist) Recycling Protocol: Friendly construct destroyed -> gain 2 Scrap and +1 AP (1/turn). Upgrade: Gain 3 Scrap.
(The Mechanist) Overclock: Constructs act twice/turn, take 1 Dmg/turn. Upgrade: Suffer 0 Dmg/turn.
(The Mechanist) Overclocked Maintenance: If you spend 1+ MOV this turn and end adjacent to a friendly construct: repair that construct for HEAL 1, CLEANSE it, and both you and it gain SHIELD 1. Upgrade: Repair HEAL 2.
(The Mechanist) Field Technician: Your end-turn repair (Blueprint Tread) can target constructs in RANGE 2. After any repair you perform, gain +1 STR on your next attack. Upgrade: +2 STR.
